# sK / short-KUT Trim Doctrine v1

Locked rule: sKs are immediate-use emotional/audio objects.

## sK approval law

APPROVE only when the sK:

- starts fast,
- carries usable meaning,
- has no music-only intro,
- has no music-only tail,
- ends cleanly after the usable moment resolves.

## Adjustment law

Mark ADJUST when:

- the intro is music-only,
- the tail is music-only,
- the usable phrase starts late,
- the usable phrase is cut off,
- the ending drifts beyond the emotional/vocal/action moment.

## Standard adjustment notes

ADJUST: trim music-only intro; start at first usable emotional/vocal/sonic moment.

ADJUST: trim music-only tail; end after phrase/moment resolves.

ADJUST: fix cutoff; do not cut phrase, final consonant, or sustaining note.

## Exception

A tiny lead or tail pad is allowed only to avoid a hard cut or protect the musical/vocal attack or final sustain.

## Audio preflight law

No audio review player may be delivered unless the referenced audio file exists and has duration greater than zero.
