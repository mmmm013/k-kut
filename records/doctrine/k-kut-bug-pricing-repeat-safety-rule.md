# BUG Pricing / Repeat / Safety Rule

## Locked public rule

A BUG is a tiny, light, affectionate reminder or nudge.

BUGs are Short-KUT-only.

BUGs may only provide eligible Short-KUTs / tiny KUTs.

BUGs may not provide full K-KUTs.

## Price rule

Every BUG is $1.99.

Flat unit price:

- 1 BUG = $1.99
- 2 BUGs = $3.98
- 3 BUGs = $5.97
- 4 BUGs = $7.96
- 5 BUGs = $9.95

No public BUG option may exceed 5 BUGs.

No 6+ BUG public option.

Bundles may be considered later, but the locked first public rule is flat $1.99 each.

## Repeat rule

Repeated BUGs are allowed only from 1x through 5x.

5x is the hard public maximum.

Past 5 starts to feel like pestering and is not allowed in the public flow.

## BUG examples

BUGs are for short reminder-like messages such as:

- Call me.
- Text me.
- Remember.
- Love you.
- Don’t forget.
- Thinking of you.
- Check in.
- Breathe.
- You got this.
- Miss you.

## Safety / tone gate

BUGs must be:

- light
- brief
- affectionate
- non-demanding
- non-threatening
- non-manipulative
- easy to receive
- easy to ignore
- easy to smile at

BUGs must not be used for:

- pressure
- guilt
- harassment
- anger
- repeated pestering
- romantic coercion
- threats
- jealousy
- surveillance
- control
- heavy apology
- grief delivery
- serious repair

## Identity rule

Correct:

BUG intent container -> Short-KUT display slot -> neutral KUT inventory ID

Wrong:

BUG-KUT-001 as canonical audio identity

The KUT remains a KUT.
The Short-KUT remains a Short-KUT.
The BUG remains an intent container.
