# K-KUT Public / Internal Naming Rule

## Locked rule

Public shoppers see simple numbered K-KUT options.

Use:

- KK1
- KK2
- KK3
- KK4
- etc.

Do not show shoppers song titles, LT-PIX titles, source names, admin titles, review names, artist/source names, BLK names, internal file names, or engineering labels on the public buying card.

## Why

The shopper should not have to understand the inventory, song source, LT-PIX, II, CI, BLK, review history, or source chain.

The shopper tells MC-BOT what the HUG should do.

MC-BOT narrows the available K-KUT options.

The shopper hears, compares, adjusts, or sends.

## Internal rule

Internally, every public KK number must map to a complete durable record.

Internal/admin metadata should preserve:

- stable product id
- internal title
- LT-PIX/source family
- source/artist/session where known
- BLK/section role where known
- audio URL
- feeling lane
- emotional intent
- size/tier
- selection notes
- release/check status
- pricing tier
- checkout target

## Holiday rule

Holiday pages may number the public options temporarily.

Holiday KK1 is a public display number inside that holiday container, not permanent ownership by the holiday.

The durable K-KUT item returns to its system home after the holiday use case.
