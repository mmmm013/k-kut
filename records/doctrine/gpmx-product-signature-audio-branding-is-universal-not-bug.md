# GPMx Product Signature Audio Branding Is Universal, Not BUG-Specific

## Correction

Padding, slight end fade, and Twinkle are not a BUG implementation.

They are GPMx Signature Audio Branding.

They apply to all release-bound GPMx audio products and delivery experiences.

## Sonic logo rule

Padding + slight end fade + Twinkle together function like a traditional brand logo.

They are the sonic brand mark.

They identify GPMx audio behavior, finish, presentation, and release quality.

## Applies to all products

This rule applies across:

- K-KUT
- Short-KUT
- HUG
- TUG
- BUG
- KUPID delivery
- private delivery
- public delivery
- review-to-release paths
- KKr to HOME registration
- all release-bound GPMx audio

BUG is only one downstream consumer.

No product owns the signature layer.

No product defines its own separate signature layer.

## SSOT rule

The required SSOT is:

`data/audio-branding/gpmx-signature-audio-branding-ssot.json`

All product lines inherit from this SSOT.

No front-end, back-end, product, theme, holiday, delivery mode, or campaign may create a separate rule.

## Required components

Every release-bound audio item must receive:

1. Required padding
2. Slight fade at KUT end
3. Twinkle / GPMx Signature Audio Branding

These are required before Release Gate / Outlet.

They are not optional.

They are not demand-based.

They are not added only when requested.

They are applied during processing intake.

## STI / BTI / BF rule

Padding, end fade, and Twinkle are STIs.

The signature layer requires STI-Slot handling, BTI definition, BF guardrail, and release/outlet approval.

## Product architecture rule

Product identity remains:

- K-KUT
- Short-KUT
- HUG
- TUG
- BUG

Signature Audio Branding is not a product family.

Signature Audio Branding is not an intent container.

Signature Audio Branding is not a BUG feature.

Signature Audio Branding is universal GPMx audio branding.

## Fail-closed release rule

Any release-bound item missing required padding, slight end fade, or Twinkle must fail closed.

It may remain in review or processing.

It must not enter public release, delivery outlet, or final product fulfillment.
