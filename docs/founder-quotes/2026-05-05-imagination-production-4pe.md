# Founder Quote — Imagination Meets Production

Date captured: May 5, 2026  
Founder: Gregory D. Putnam  
Company: G Putnam Music, LLC  
Context: K-KUT / 4PE / KKr / HUG operating doctrine

## Quote

“It must run perfectly, which, in an operation entirely controlled here by you and me only, means we are all that stops us within the edges of technology, where imagination meets production and production meets imagination.”

## Founder Note

This was not written as a prepared statement. It came out naturally while striving to keep 4PE in all its brilliant simplicity interwoven with humane philosophy, persistently shared from Day 1 in February 2025.

## Standing Principle

Allow imagination, creativity, and charitableness to spread for all.

## Product Meaning

K-KUT, HUG, KKr, and 4PE are not only technical systems. They are mechanisms for turning human feeling, original music, and purposeful delivery into usable, caring, shareable audio experiences.

The work must be simple enough for users, structured enough for production, and humane enough to honor the reason it exists.
