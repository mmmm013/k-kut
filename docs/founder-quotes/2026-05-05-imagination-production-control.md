# Founder Quote — Imagination, Production, and Control

Date captured: May 5, 2026  
Founder: Gregory D. Putnam  
Company: G Putnam Music, LLC

## Quote

“It must run perfectly, which, in an operation entirely controlled here by you and me only, means we are all that stops us within the edges of technology, where imagination meets production and production meets imagination.”

## Expanded Founder Intent

This was not written for purpose. It came out while striving to keep 4PE in all its brilliant simplicity interwoven with humane philosophy as persistently shared from Day 1 in February 2025.

Allow imagination, creativity, and charitableness to spread for all.

## Operating Meaning

4PE must be simple on the surface and powerful underneath.

K-KUT and HUG must feel human, guided, and usable.

Production must serve imagination.

Imagination must become production.

Control exists to protect user comfort, product clarity, and humane purpose.
