# HUGs & TUGs — K-KUT Product Language

## Core Meaning

HUGs and TUGs are K-KUT sentiment products.

A HUG carries warmth, care, gratitude, love, comfort, support, appreciation, celebration, tenderness, peace, and encouragement.

A TUG carries longing, apology, repair, grief, regret, distance, hard feelings, unresolved emotion, missing someone, and the pull to reconnect.

## Core Line

HUGs & TUGs — real music moments for real feelings.

## Public Explanation

Some feelings need a HUG.
Some feelings need a TUG.

A HUG holds them.
A TUG pulls them closer.

Both are private K-KUT links that let someone send a real music moment when words are not enough.

## Product Control

HUGs and TUGs are controlled K-KUT pages/links, not downloadable audio files.

The buyer may share the private link by email, DM, social, or text from their own phone.

The raw K-KUT audio file is not delivered as a download unless GPM intentionally sells a separate full-song download product.

## HUG Categories

HUGs include:

- Mother’s Day
- Thank You
- Appreciation
- Love
- Encouragement
- Celebration
- Comfort
- Support
- Birthday
- Anniversary
- Holiday greetings

## TUG Categories

TUGs include:

- I Miss You
- I’m Sorry
- Please Understand
- Come Back
- Hard Feelings
- Longing
- Grief / Comfort
- Repair
- Regret
- Distance

## Mother’s Day Rule

Mother’s Day remains a HUG product.

Do not rename Mother’s Day as HUGs & TUGs.

Use:

Send Mom a real audio HUG.

TUGs may be teased as a coming or deeper-feeling K-KUT category, but Mother’s Day sales stay focused on HUGs.

## Voice Rule

MC-BOT leads HUG and TUG product flows.

GP-BOT is reserved only for intentional Founder / Founder Quote clicks or links.

## Display Rule

Do not display all available KKs at once.

Show one section or one curated set at a time.

The system may use many KKs internally through KKr metadata, but the user experience remains guided, simple, and emotionally focused.
