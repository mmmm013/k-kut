# GPMx KUT Finishing Standard

## Status

Operating standard for finished KUTs, HUGs, and Wedding Pack section options.

This document exists because a KUT is not just a chopped audio section. A KUT is a finished, listenable, emotionally complete product unit.

## Core Rule

A KUT is not approved until it sounds complete.

A finished KUT must have:

1. phrase-complete musical content
2. correct front padding
3. correct back padding
4. clean natural boundary or approved fade
5. no clipped first word
6. no clipped final line
7. no abrupt emotional cutoff
8. appropriate display name
9. buyer-facing intent
10. proof through listening review

## Product Definition

A KUT is a governed, pre-approved song moment derived from a source PIX.

A customer may choose an approved KUT. A customer may not rearrange sections, stitch non-contiguous sections, or build custom edits.

## Padding Standard

Padding is part of the KUT product definition.

Padding means the KUT does not start or end exactly at a guessed lyric boundary. It includes enough musical lead-in and tail for the moment to breathe.

### Front Padding

Front padding provides musical context before the vocal or target section begins.

Default range:

- 1.5 to 3.0 seconds

Use more front padding when:

- the section begins abruptly
- the vocal enters immediately
- the emotional setup needs a musical breath
- the previous chord resolves into the chosen section

Use less front padding when:

- the section has a clean instrumental pickup
- the previous material distracts from the selected moment
- the KUT is meant to feel immediate

### Back Padding

Back padding preserves the final lyric and musical resolution.

Default range:

- 3.0 to 6.0 seconds

Use more back padding when:

- the final lyric needs to land emotionally
- the band holds a chord
- the phrase resolves after the lyric ends
- the ending feels chopped without tail

Use less back padding when:

- the next section begins immediately and would confuse the KUT
- the target KUT must remain short
- there is a clean natural stop

## Fade Standard

Fades are allowed only when they improve the listening experience.

A fade must not hide a bad cut.

Default ranges:

- fade in: 0.25 to 0.50 seconds
- fade out: 1.5 to 3.0 seconds

Use fade-in when:

- the cut begins on sustained sound
- the first beat would click or pop
- the entry needs polish

Use fade-out when:

- the section does not have a natural ending
- the musical tail is good but needs a gentle finish
- the next section begins before the selected KUT should continue

Do not use fade-out if it cuts off a lyric, hook, title phrase, or emotional payoff.

## Phrase-Completion Standard

A KUT must preserve the complete musical and emotional thought.

For lyric-based KUTs, the KUT must not end before the resolving lyric.

Examples of resolving lines in Forever & A Day may include:

- forever and a day
- our love will remain
- the book we started yesterday
- forever, forever and a day

A section cut fails review if:

- it cuts off the final lyric
- it cuts before the hook lands
- it ends before the chord/phrase resolves
- the listener feels interrupted
- it sounds like an edit instead of a product

## Twinkle Standard

Twinkle is a presentation treatment, not a casual effect.

Twinkle may mean:

1. a page/delivery sparkle cue
2. a visual/audio opening cue in the experience
3. a short branded stinger
4. a gentle post-play sparkle

### Current Rule

Use Page Twinkle first.

Do not burn audio Twinkle into KUT files until an approved Twinkle audio asset and rule set exist.

### Page Twinkle

Page Twinkle may include:

- subtle visual sparkle
- premium reveal animation
- soft confirmation cue
- recipient opening moment
- polished HUG presentation

Page Twinkle should support the song, not compete with it.

### Audio Twinkle

Audio Twinkle is not approved by default.

Audio Twinkle requires:

1. approved Twinkle audio asset
2. approved use cases
3. approved placement rule
4. A/B review against clean KUT
5. no interference with source music rights or emotional intent

## KUT Review Checklist

Each KUT must pass listening review:

- [ ] first sound is clean
- [ ] first lyric is not clipped
- [ ] section starts musically
- [ ] final line is complete
- [ ] emotional payoff is present
- [ ] ending has enough tail
- [ ] fade feels natural if used
- [ ] no unrelated section leaks in
- [ ] volume is acceptable
- [ ] title matches what the user hears
- [ ] option is not fake or duplicated
- [ ] checkout/fulfillment can preserve this exact KUT

## Display Standard

Public option cards must feel like finished products, not lab labels.

Bad labels:

- v1
- ch1
- cut 3
- source-backed candidate
- draft section option

Better working labels:

- Opening Vow Moment
- First Forever Chorus
- Forever Promise
- Bridge Into Forever
- Final Forever Chorus
- Forever & A Day Closing Moment

Internal section codes may exist in metadata, but public labels should be buyer-friendly.

## Status Rules

Use these states:

- `draft_cut`
- `padded_draft`
- `phrase_complete_review`
- `twinkle_page_ready`
- `audio_verified`
- `approved_kut`
- `launch_ready`
- `checkout_ready`
- `rejected`

A KUT may not be checkout-ready until it is at least:

- `audio_verified`
- `approved_kut`
- `launch_ready`

## Forever & A Day Wedding Pack Application

The Wedding Song Promo uses one approved source PIX:

- Forever & A Day

The Wedding Pack may include multiple KUTs from that one PIX only.

Possible KUT options:

- Intro / opening moment
- Verse 1
- First chorus
- Verse 1 + first chorus
- Verse 2 + chorus
- Bridge into final chorus
- Final chorus
- Final chorus + outro

Each option must be cut as a phrase-complete, padded, listenable product. No option may simply point to the full song unless it is explicitly labeled as full reference audio.

## Control Rule

No KUT is public-ready just because the file exists.

A file becomes a KUT only when it passes:

1. source match
2. section definition
3. phrase completion
4. padding/fade review
5. listening review
6. metadata review
7. launch seeding
8. checkout preservation

## Mantra

No clipped lines.
No fake choices.
No dry chops.
No checkout without fulfillment.

A KUT must feel complete enough to send.
