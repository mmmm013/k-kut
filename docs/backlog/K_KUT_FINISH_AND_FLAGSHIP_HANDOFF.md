# K-KUT Finish + Flagship Domain Handoff

Status: ACTIVE

## Core Public Rule

Lead with HUG, not invention language.

Users care first about:
- understanding the offer
- choosing the feeling
- hearing the audio
- sending privately
- trusting the path

Invention language is authority/proof/support, not the first public offer.

## Always-On K-KUT / HUG Structure

K-KUT.com remains the always-running HUG entry.

Public paths:
- Personal
- Holiday

Personal and Holiday are isolated user paths.

They may share the same best-match audio internally when the emotional match is strongest.

## Candidate Rule

Candidate boxes must say HUG, not K-KUT / KUT / kut.

Remove no-value-added technical copy from cards:
- “Locked Thank You text-structure K-KUT from SSOT”
- raw internal structure names when they do not help the user

Keep:
- HUG 1 of 9
- user-friendly feeling title
- audio preview
- send path

## Audio Rule

Only one audio preview may play at a time.

KK/HUG preview audio:
- no padding
- no Twinkle embedded
- no branding tail
- no spoken tag

Tiny microfade/click protection is allowed only to prevent harsh cutoffs.

Signature Twinkle is separate closing cue research, not baked into KK/HUG masters.

## MC-BOT Rule

MC-BOT stays.

MC-BOT acts as guide, usher, shield, and complaint handler.

MC-BOT can help a user choose, explain, recover, or route.

MC-BOT should not clutter the tiny HUG definition, but it must remain visible as the helpful guide.
