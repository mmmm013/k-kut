# GPMx LineFeel Law

## Definition

A LineFeel is a 2-line or 3-line lyric snippet that carries clear emotional meaning.

LineFeels are found by reading the full lyric plainly and ignoring official song structure.

LineFeels are not K-KUTs.

LineFeels are not mKs.

LineFeels are text/meaning candidates used by KKR, MC-BOT, reports, and future product selection.

## Public Name

Use:

- Feeling Lines

Do not use publicly:

- mK
- mini-KUT
- Line-KK
- KK-Feel
- CC
- internal IDs

## Purpose

LineFeels identify:

- clear feeling
- positive meaning
- recipient-safe language
- ceremony-ready statements
- symbolic images
- microcosms
- idioms / expressions
- emotionally useful 2-line or 3-line units

## Use

LineFeels may support:

- Short KUT candidate discovery
- HUG recommendation copy
- MC-BOT guidance
- recipient DP captions
- social/share text
- product descriptions
- backend monitoring and reports

## Control Rule

Approving a LineFeel does not approve a K-KUT.

A LineFeel only means:

This lyric snippet has emotional and ceremonial value worth reviewing.

## Required Fields

Each LineFeel record should include:

- pix_id
- pix_title
- source_lyric_path
- line_start
- line_end
- line_count
- text_snippet
- feeling_tags
- celebration_tags
- genre_tag
- positive_meaning_score
- clarity_score
- imagery_score
- recipient_safety_score
- ceremony_fit_score
- total_score_10
- risk_tags
- review_status
- review_notes

## Scoring

Use a 10-point control score:

- Feeling clarity: 0–2
- Positive / recipient-safe meaning: 0–2
- Imagery / symbolism / microcosm value: 0–2
- Ceremony fit: 0–2
- Reuse / product support value: 0–2

## Rating

- 9–10 = excellent
- 7–8 = strong
- 5–6 = possible
- 3–4 = triage
- 0–2 = reject


## Pricing Rule

LineFeels are not sold as text snippets.

If a LineFeel later becomes an approved audio K-KUT, use mid-price by default.

Default:

- Meaningful 2-line / 3-line audio K-KUT from a LineFeel = HUG / mid-price
- Very short phrase-only audio cut = Short KUT / low price
- Expanded emotional arc from a LineFeel = Big HUG / high price

Pricing is applied only after separate audio-cut approval.

## Frontend Display

Frontend may show approved LineFeels as valuable text snippets called Feeling Lines.

Frontend must not present them as K-KUTs unless an audio cut has been separately approved.

## KKR Rule

KKR must look blankly at the full lyric and find 2-line and 3-line sets that convey clear feeling.

Official PIX structure is ignored for this strategy.

Meaning comes first.
