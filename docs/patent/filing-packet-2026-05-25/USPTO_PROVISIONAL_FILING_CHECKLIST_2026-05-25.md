# USPTO Provisional Filing Checklist — K-KUT

## Filing target

U.S. provisional patent application.

## Documents to prepare

- Specification / disclosure PDF.
- Optional drawings / figures PDF.
- Provisional application cover sheet.
- Fee payment.
- Filing receipt saved to repo/private records.

## Required internal content

- Title.
- Inventor/applicant information.
- Background.
- Summary.
- Definitions.
- Detailed description.
- Embodiments.
- Operational gates.

## USPTO portal steps

1. Log into USPTO Patent Center.
2. Start new application.
3. Select provisional utility application.
4. Enter applicant/inventor data.
5. Upload specification PDF.
6. Upload drawings PDF if available.
7. Upload provisional cover sheet if required by workflow.
8. Review fee status: regular / small entity / micro entity.
9. Pay filing fee.
10. Save filing receipt immediately.
11. Record application number and filing date in invention log.
12. Calendar nonprovisional deadline: 12 months from filing date.

## After filing

- Mark appropriate materials as Patent Pending only after successful filing.
- Do not claim patent granted.
- Preserve filing receipt.
- Start nonprovisional claim drafting immediately.
