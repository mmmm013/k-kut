# Holiday Dynamic Assembly Lock

**Authority:** GD / Greg Putnam
**Locked:** 2026-07-18
**Status:** Mandatory GPMx / K-KUT product law

## Permanent rule

Holiday is one dynamic Theme.

There are no permanent named-holiday packages, collections, inventories, storefronts, routes, campaigns, presets, gateways, pricing systems, or shelf products.

This applies to every named holiday, including Mother’s Day, Father’s Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Easter, and all others.

## Only permanent holiday mechanism

The generic Holiday Builder / Manager is the only permanent holiday mechanism.

## Assembly triggers

A holiday collection may be assembled only:

1. when its applicable season requires it; or
2. when a user explicitly requests it.

## Assembly law

Every holiday collection is assembled fresh for its specific season, event, recipient, and request.

Eligible IIs remain part of general inventory. They do not become permanent named-holiday inventory.

No named-holiday collection exists before assembly is required.

No named-holiday collection remains on the shelf after the seasonal or user-requested purpose ends.

## Runtime law

Permanent named-holiday routes must not exist.

Unknown named-holiday URLs must return 404.

The general `/holiday` Holiday Builder / Manager route remains available.

## Gate law

A build or release must be blocked if permanent named-holiday runtime, routes, packages, collections, campaigns, or stored holiday-specific product machinery reappears.
