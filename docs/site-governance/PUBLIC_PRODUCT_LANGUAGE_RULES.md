# K-KUT Public Product Language Rules

## Purpose

Public buyers should see simple product words they can understand, remember, and repeat.

Internal invention terms may remain in patent, governance, processor, routing, and admin documentation, but buyer pages should use mainstream product language.

## Public Words

Use these public words:

- kut
- kuts
- KUT
- KUTs
- HUG
- HUGs
- TUG
- TUGs
- Keepsake Page

## Internal Terms Not For Buyer Pages

Do not show these terms on buyer-facing pages unless specifically approved:

- KK
- mK
- KPD
- LLF
- KKr
- 4PE processor
- source pool
- candidate inventory
- routing logic
- internal inventory

## Public Meaning

A kut is a short song moment someone can choose, buy, send, save, or personalize.

A HUG is a delivered emotional audio greeting built around a kut.

A TUG is a timed or triggered HUG delivered for a planned moment.

A Keepsake Page is an optional private page for a HUG. It may hold the kut, message, delivery moment, and recipient-safe experience.

## KPD Public Rule

KPD is an internal term.

Public buyer pages should call it Keepsake Page.

Preferred buyer phrase:

Add a Keepsake Page to this HUG.

Supporting buyer phrase:

A private page your recipient can open, save, and revisit.

## Transaction Placement

The Keepsake Page should be visible as an add-on, not hidden and not forced.

Recommended placement:

1. Pick a kut.
2. Choose the HUG.
3. Add message, recipient, and delivery details.
4. Offer add-ons, including Keepsake Page.
5. Checkout.

## Buyer-Safe Flow

Use this public flow:

Pick a kut.
Add your message.
Send a HUG.
Optional: add a Keepsake Page.
Optional: choose timed delivery as a TUG.

## Doctrine

Public language sells the feeling.

Internal language protects the invention.
