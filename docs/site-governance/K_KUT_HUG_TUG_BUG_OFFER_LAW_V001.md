# K-KUT HUG / TUG / BUG Offer Law V001

Status: GD-authorized development lock

## Source lineage

All customer IIs remain traceable derivatives of the canonical 429 LT-PIX source set. Customer-facing package names do not replace the permanent source, audio, registry, or II identity.

## Customer offers

| Canonical II form | Customer offer | Price | Discovery placement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| KK or KOMBO | HUG | $7.99 | May appear inside one or more of the 13 HUGz Cards when sentiment-matched |
| sK | TUG | $4.99 | Separate TUG lane; never placed inside a HUGz Card |
| mK | BUG | $1.99 | Separate BUG lane; never placed inside a HUGz Card |

## BUG source restriction

A BUG may use only an authorized mK sourced from one of these forms:

- TRM
- XCLM
- VSND

No other source form is eligible for a BUG.

## HUGz Card identity

A HUGz Card is a sentiment-selection container.

A HUGz Card:

- is not an II;
- is not audio or media;
- is not the purchased customer product;
- houses only sentiment-matched HUG choices;
- houses only KKs and KOMBOs;
- presents three choices at a time;
- may continue with another three choices while staying inside the selected card theme;
- may be used as a primary, promotional, alternate-domain, or emergency-backup presentation of the same governed HUG inventory.

The selected KK or KOMBO is packaged and purchased as one HUG for $7.99.

## Income-first operating rule

Revenue work may use the existing correct Stripe payment route even when its historic display title is not yet normalized, provided that:

- the customer sees the correct price;
- the exact selected II remains attached to the order;
- fulfillment receives the exact selected-II reference;
- the correct finished customer audio is delivered;
- no obsolete $12.99 offer is displayed or sold.

Stripe naming cleanup must not delay a correct $7.99 HUG or $4.99 TUG sale.

## Cross-domain rule

The 13 HUGz Cards may be shown on approved G Putnam Music domains, but all presentations must point to the same underlying II identity and payment/fulfillment authority. A domain presentation must not create a duplicate II or a competing inventory record.

## Current development boundary

This law changes customer-facing semantics and selection presentation. It does not authorize:

- source-audio mutation;
- customer-audio mutation;
- new audio rendering;
- public inventory rewriting;
- Stripe product creation;
- Stripe price creation;
- Production deployment.
