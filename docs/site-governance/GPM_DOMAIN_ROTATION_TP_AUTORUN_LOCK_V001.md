# GPM Domain Rotation TP Autorun Lock V001

Status: GD-directed, adopted, and locked on 2026-07-17.

## Canonical tool

GPM LT-PIX TP Review.

## Required TP autorun law

Every candidate considered for the K-KUT / Sent-i-Meant domain rotation must automatically enter the existing TP validation and review-set process after controlled source proof. A candidate must not remain merely labeled `TP_REQUIRED` or be deferred without creating its TP set.

The required method is a two-layer musical-to-vocal alignment process.

### Layer 1 — musical structure authority

1. Confirm the controlled WAV/lossless LT-PIX source, identity binding, hash, duration, channels, and source rights.
2. Find and bind the associated IN-PIX or INO-PIX musical version where one exists.
3. Use that musical version to identify and validate the real musical transition points between sections.
4. Record those Musical TPs as structural anchors, including intentional N/A decisions where a conventional section does not exist.
5. Musical TPs are candidates only. They do not automatically become final vocal boundaries.

### Layer 2 — LT-PIX vocal authority

6. Compare each Musical TP against the authoritative LT-PIX and find the matching musical transition.
7. From the matching Musical TP, trace forward on the LT-PIX to the first complete audible vocal onset for the section start.
8. From the following Musical TP, music-only continuation, or silence, trace backward on the LT-PIX to the final audible vocal sound and natural release for the section end.
9. Do not cut a word, syllable, held vowel, breath, vocal decay, or natural resolve.
10. Do not assume an immediate next-BLK transition is the last vocal sound.
11. Do not begin at `0:00` unless the vocal actually begins at `0:00`.
12. The vocal-bounded LT-PIX interval becomes the proposed KK/sK section.

## Authority rule

The associated IN-PIX/INO-PIX teaches and validates musical structure. The LT-PIX SSOT controls the actual KK/sK audio, vocal boundaries, and final timing.

An INO-PIX may support Musical TP learning and alignment, but it must not be used as a lyric KK source. KKs and contiguous KK-KOMBOS must be cut from the same authoritative LT-PIX and must not be assembled from separately rendered fragments.

## Required automatic sequence

1. Confirm controlled source proof and LT-PIX identity.
2. Bind associated IN-PIX/INO-PIX where available.
3. Run source/hash/duration/channel/silence validation.
4. Generate the Musical TP map.
5. Align Musical TPs to the LT-PIX.
6. Generate proposed forward-traced vocal starts and backward-traced vocal ends.
7. Run TP ordering, overlap, gap, endpoint, and natural-resolve consistency checks.
8. Generate or refresh the ordered GPM LT-PIX TP Review set.
9. Open the source-first human review, not a derivative KK/section file.
10. Record human TP marks, N/A decisions, corrections, reviewer, and completion status.
11. Feed the completed TP record into KKr blank-slate KK/sK derivation.
12. Record passes, failures, corrections, confidence, and what changed as learning evidence.
13. Auto-advance to the next candidate only; never auto-approve final inventory.

## Pilot control

`data/site-governance/gpm-domain-feeling-rotation-tpr-set-v001.csv` is the current ordered set manifest. The Foundation is first for immediate review after the candidate list is completed.

## Learning requirement

Every paired comparison must preserve:

- the associated musical source used;
- Musical TP proposals;
- matched LT-PIX transition locations;
- forward distance to first vocal onset;
- backward distance to final vocal end-sound;
- human corrections;
- accepted and rejected TP proposals;
- confidence and failure-point evidence.

This evidence must improve the next automatic TP proposal set. A review that does not preserve learning evidence is incomplete.

## Final inventory controls remain separate

TP completion does not prove final II readiness. Final II still requires approved audible boundaries, natural resolve, canonical GPMx Twinkle at end, registry proof, buyer-safe copy, correct route, live click-to-play proof, and Production BIC PASS.

## Forbidden

- Approval from title or theme alone.
- Stopping at `TP_REQUIRED` without creating the TP set.
- Skipping TP because a WAV or parent map exists.
- Treating a Musical TP as automatically equal to a vocal boundary.
- Using an INSTRO as a lyric KK source.
- Cutting before the first complete vocal onset or before the last vocal end-sound resolves.
- Treating an automatically proposed TP as human approval.
- Replacing the existing GPM LT-PIX TP Review with a new workflow.
- Production deployment from this documentation lock.
