**Production deployment authorized by this document:** NO

## 1. Adopted Outcome

GPM will use one controlled, rolling presentation of approved KKs, sKs, and approved SWSPs across the existing domains. This is not a new product. It extends the existing Sent-i-Meant discovery, K-KUT selection/purchase, and GPMx source/catalog roles.

The pilot must help a customer:

1. recognize the feeling or occasion;
2. hear a small number of approved moments;
3. choose the moment that says it correctly;
4. continue into the existing K-KUT HUG purchase and delivery path.

## 2. Locked Domain Division

### `sentimeant.com` — feeling and occasion discovery

- Canonical rolling discovery screen.
- Public question: **What do you mean to share?**
- Shows feeling/occasion lanes and short buyer-friendly cues.
- Selecting a moment sends the customer to its approved playable K-KUT selection.
- No internal KK, sK, SWSP, INO-PIX, TP, registry, or manufacturing language is public.
- No automatic audio playback.

### `sentimeants.com` — canonical entry alias

- Must not operate a competing rotation, catalog, or second source of truth.
- Leads to the canonical `sentimeant.com` discovery experience.
- Does not create consent, inventory, pricing, or independent product law.

### `k-kut.com` — hear, choose, purchase, and deliver

- Holds the actual approved playable customer cards.
- Each card contains only:
  - buyer-friendly feeling title;
  - one short meaning line;
  - click-to-play audio;
  - **Send This Feeling** action;
  - existing approved K-KUT HUG purchase/delivery path.
- No autoplay.
- No candidate may appear until all audio and release gates pass.

### `gputnammusic.com` — source, artist, and catalog authority

- Presents the real PIX/INO-PIX title, artist/source identity, studio/artwork context, and creative history.
- Hosts Michael Scherer and Michael Clay source/artist spotlights.
- May link an approved source moment to the appropriate Sent-i-Meant lane and K-KUT card.
- Does not replace K-KUT as the customer selection and delivery domain.

## 3. Locked Pilot Lanes

The pilot uses seven lanes.

1. **I’m Sorry / Repair**
   - Intentionally smaller and quieter than the other lanes.
   - Approximately three public lines: the feeling cue, one short meaning line, and one action.
   - Never presented as pressure, blame, or forced reconciliation.

2. **Thank You / Appreciation**
   - Gratitude, recognition, friendship, family, support, loyalty, and appreciation.

3. **Romance & Sexy**
   - Warm, intimate, playful, romantic, and adult while remaining tasteful and emotionally legible.

4. **Courage / Overcoming**
   - Strength, recovery, survival, persistence, belief, confidence, and hope.

5. **Awesome Anniversary**
   - Uses approved **Awesome Anniversary sKs** and other approved anniversary-compatible moments.
   - Celebration of commitment, shared history, loyalty, love, and partnership.

6. **Best Birthday**
   - Uses approved **Best Birthday sKs** and other approved birthday-compatible moments.
   - Celebration, affection, recognition, encouragement, humor, and personal appreciation.

7. **Comfort / Sympathy / Grief**
   - This is the major human theme missing from the earlier pilot set and is now adopted.
   - Comfort, remembrance, presence, loss, grief, support, and “you are not alone.”
   - Must remain gentle and must never exploit grief or use sensational imagery/copy.

### Theme coverage conclusion

The seven lanes cover the major pilot needs:

- repair;
- gratitude;
- intimacy;
- courage;
- anniversary;
- birthday;
- comfort/grief.

Friendship and family live primarily under Thank You. Congratulations and general milestones may be tested later but are not required as a separate pilot lane.

## 5. Locked Rolling-Screen Behavior

- Slow, calm rotation; no frantic carousel behavior.
- One primary featured moment at a time.
- Three to five supporting choices may remain visible.
- Manual arrows/swipe controls.
- Rotation pauses on hover, focus, or touch interaction.
- Audio never starts automatically.
- Customer category choice is retained while browsing.
- “I’m Sorry” remains a smaller three-line feature.
- Birthday and Anniversary remain distinct occasion lanes, not buried inside Romance.
- Comfort/Grief remains distinct from I’m Sorry because loss is not an apology.

## 6. Four-Week Representation Rule

The recurring event is adopted as the **Friday Feeling Drop**.

- Cadence: once each Friday.
- Exact clock time: **HELD FOR GD SELECTION**; no time may be invented.
- A weekly drop changes only the approved featured selections, not product law, price, checkout, or delivery.
- The public screen should not attempt to show every source title at once.

Across every rolling four-week window, the approved feature schedule must prove representation of:

- all seven pilot lanes;
- Michael Scherer source/SWSP material;
- Michael Clay piano/SWSP material;
- vocal and instrumental/piano presentation;
- older and newer controlled source material;
- gratitude, repair, intimacy, courage, celebration, and comfort;
- at least one Awesome Anniversary sK;
- at least one Best Birthday sK.

No lane may monopolize the screen for the entire four-week window.

## 7. Public Card Law

Every public playable card must use:

1. a buyer-friendly feeling/occasion title;
2. one brief meaning line;
3. click-to-play audio;
4. one clear action;
5. existing approved product and delivery law.

Public cards must not expose:

- KK, sK, SWSP, INO-PIX, TP, BLK, KKr, MIAL, SSOT, or registry language;
- source file paths;
- internal review status;
- unapproved artist metadata;
- unapproved audio or external audio.
