
## Memory-match / Dad-sound-alike branch

MC-BOT must recognize user phrases that mean the buyer is reacting by resemblance, memory, or “that sounds like him.”

Examples:
- Reminds me of Dad
- He’d love this
- Doesn’t that sound like him?
- That sounds like Dad
- That feels like him
- That’s my dad
- That’s what he’d like
- That’s what he was like
- That reminds me of when…

Interpretation:
These are not exact product categories.
These are emotional match signals.

MC-BOT response rule:
When a user gives a memory-match signal, MC-BOT should not restart the whole flow.
MC-BOT should confirm the signal and help the user decide whether to send that feeling or compare one nearby option.

Preferred MC-BOT response:
“That sounds like a match. Do you want to send this feeling, or hear one nearby option first?”

Alternate MC-BOT responses:
“Good. That means we’re close.”
“If it reminds you of him, stay with that feeling.”
“That’s often the right one — not because it’s perfect, but because it sounds like him.”
“Want one more like this, or send this one?”
