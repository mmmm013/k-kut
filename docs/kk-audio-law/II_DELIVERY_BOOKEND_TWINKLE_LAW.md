# II Delivery Bookend + Twinkle Law

Status: LOCKED

## Raw source law

Raw KK / structure audio remains the source truth.

Raw structure audio must not be renamed, confused, or replaced by dressed delivery audio.

## Customer delivery law

Every customer-delivered II / HUG audio object must include:

1. front bookend padding
2. back bookend padding
3. Signature End Sound / Twinkle

This applies to all IIs.

## Separation rule

Raw KK source audio and customer delivery audio are not the same artifact.

Raw KK:
- proves the text / lyric / structure item
- remains clean
- remains source-bound

II delivery audio:
- is what the customer hears
- must be presentation-safe
- must prevent harsh cutoffs
- must include Signature End Sound / Twinkle

## No confusion rule

Do not call raw KK audio “final customer delivery” unless it has passed II delivery assembly.

Do not bake random branding, spoken tags, CCs, mKs, micros, Sandman, or magic tails into the II.

Only the approved Signature End Sound / Twinkle may close the II.
