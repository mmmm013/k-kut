# Fatherhood Feelings and KK Review Doctrine

Status: CURRENT WORKING DOCTRINE

## Core law

Father’s Day KKs must be reviewed through actual fatherhood feelings, not shallow holiday labels.

A Dad KK may fit more than one theme.

A Dad KK may fit both living-dad and deceased-dad contexts if the exact audio supports both.

A Dad KK may also be excluded from one context while fitting another.

The exact KK audio decides.

## Living Dad emotional fields

Living Dad KKs may express:

- respect
- strength
- loyalty
- dedication
- toughness
- hope
- gratitude
- perseverance
- patience
- protection
- guidance
- sacrifice
- steadiness
- humor
- pride
- forgiveness
- repair
- presence
- legacy-in-progress
- admiration
- dependable love
- hard work
- quiet care

## Deceased / absent Dad emotional fields

Deceased, absent, or remembered Dad KKs may express:

- remembrance
- grief
- missing
- empty chair
- unfinished words
- gratitude after loss
- honor
- legacy
- strength remembered
- protection remembered
- regret
- peace
- hope
- spiritual comfort
- family continuation
- “you’re still with me”
- tenderness
- longing
- goodbye without finality

## Review angles

Each KK should be reviewed by angle, POV, and use case.

Possible angles:

- Thanks for guiding me
- You made me stronger
- I still miss you
- I remember your chair / place / voice
- You carried us
- You taught me patience
- You stayed tough
- You showed up
- I could not say it then
- I want to honor you now
- I see your legacy in me / us
- You were not perfect, but this is careful
- I need repair without pressure

## POV

Possible POVs:

- child to living father
- child to deceased father
- adult child to father
- spouse/partner honoring father
- family honoring Dad
- friend honoring a father figure
- stepchild to stepdad
- sender to absent dad
- sender to complicated dad
- sender remembering dad privately
- recipient self-keep / keepsake

## KK review rule

A KK must be tagged only by what the exact audio supports.

Do not tag by PIX title alone.

Do not tag by holiday lane alone.

Do not tag by full-song meaning if the excerpt does not carry that meaning.

## Silo rule

All KKs can exist in multiple Father’s Day silos.

Silos rank existing KKs by:

- relevance
- priority
- emotional safety
- living/deceased fit
- use case
- POV
- delivery readiness
- user clarity

## MSP rule

Father’s Day MSP must help the user find the right Dad feeling.

It must not manipulate.

It must not force a “happy Father’s Day” tone when the user needs grief, repair, remembrance, or quiet respect.

MC-BOT asks the user what kind of Dad moment they are trying to send and narrows from there.

## Locked instruction

Explore ideas among all KKs.

Use Dad feelings, not title loops.

Keep PIX metadata, KK metadata, and theme/silo mappings separate.

Each KK remains connected to PIX in backend.

Buyer-facing display uses theme/use-case labels.
