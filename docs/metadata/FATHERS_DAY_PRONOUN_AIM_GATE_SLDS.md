# Father's Day Pronoun-Aim Gate — SLDS?

## SLDS?

SLDS? = Sensible? Logical? Doable? Suitable?

A KK can exist once and serve multiple use lanes when the fit is right. The system must not duplicate an LT-PIX-derived KK as a separate solo KK. One KK can carry multiple metadata uses.

## Known specific Father’s Day ban

KK STATUS:
V2b = valid existing KK

FATHER’S DAY STATUS:
V2b = FD-BAN because “her/she” recipient language makes it wrong for Father’s Day

OTHER USE STATUS:
V2b can still be used for Mother’s Day / Personal / Gratitude / Family / Support / Legacy if the emotional fit is right.

## Rule

Do not ban an entire LT-PIX because one KK fails a gender-aimed holiday gate.

For LT-PIX Thank You:
- V2b is banned from Father’s Day use.
- Other Thank You KKs are not automatically banned.
- Each KK must be reviewed individually by audio, text, theme, pronoun/recipient aim, and SLDS?.

## Holiday pronoun-aim gate

For gender-aimed holidays, KKr must detect whether the KK is aimed at the right recipient.

Father’s Day danger signals:
- her
- she
- mother
- mom
- mum
- mama
- wife
- daughter
- sister

Mother’s Day danger signals:
- he
- him
- his
- father
- dad
- husband
- son
- brother

Neutral-safe signals:
- you
- your
- they
- family
- someone
- the one who kept going

A third-person pronoun does not automatically ban a KK.
It only bans that holiday-use pairing when the pronoun points the emotional praise toward the wrong gendered recipient.
