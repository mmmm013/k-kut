# BIC-Level Operating Rules

## Rule 1 — User First

Every screen must answer:

1. What am I doing now?
2. Why does this choice matter?
3. What do I click next?
4. Can I go back, ask for help, or ask for another set?

## Rule 2 — One Focus

One step at a time.  
One question at a time.  
One primary action at a time.

## Rule 3 — Fit Beats Quantity

Never show unrelated choices to make the product look larger.

Show fewer fitting choices before showing many weak ones.

## Rule 4 — Depth Must Be Real

The system may have many options, but user-visible choices must be emotionally logical.

Broad → narrow → finer → fitting.

## Rule 5 — Audio Must Be Reliable

Any Play click:

1. Stops every other audio.
2. Resets every other audio to 0.
3. Starts selected audio from 0.

No overlapping audio.

## Rule 6 — No Internal Leakage

Never show users:

- 18A
- internal script codes
- raw process labels
- ASCAP explanations in selection flow
- audit words
- developer terms

## Rule 7 — Guide, Do Not Clutter

BOT/Guide content must lead the next action.

No decorative BOT badges.  
No giant help panels unless user asks.  
No redundant boxes.

## Rule 8 — Buyer Path Must Always Exist

If a user wants to buy now, they must have a clear live path.

Today:
Buy live Mother’s Day HUG now → Mother’s Day HUG → Checkout.

## Rule 9 — Hold Means Hold

If family, partner, artist, sponsor, or stakeholder activity is placed on hold, all related outreach and publishing stops until explicit approval.

## Rule 10 — Imagination and Production Must Meet

The system exists where imagination meets production and production meets imagination.

Creativity must become usable.
Production must stay humane.
