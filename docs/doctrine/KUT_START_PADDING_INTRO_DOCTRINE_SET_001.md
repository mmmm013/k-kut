# KUT Start / Padding / Musical INTRO Doctrine — SET 001

Status: APPROVED  
Approved by: GD Putnam  
Date: 2026-06-21  
Scope: LT-PIX-derived KUTs, with immediate enforcement on mKs and sKs.

## Core law

Clean starts. Protected endings. Parent identity untouched. Clone-level correction only.

Remove contamination, not meaning.

Preserve intentional Musical INTRO when it adds value.

Offer Clean Start / With Music INTRO only when both reviewed clone variants exist.

## Default product focus

Default = no Musical INTRO.

K-KUT sells sentimental words with music background.

The words, phrase, vocal event, hook, or emotional message are the foreground product. Music supports the message. Music does not lead the buyer away from the words by default.

## Critical distinction

No Musical INTRO does not mean no music.

No Musical INTRO means no pre-word musical lead-in by default.

Music may remain under, around, and behind the words. Music may support the vocal. Music may continue after the words when the tail is emotionally or musically necessary.

Clean Start does not mean abrupt start.

## Clean Start rule

Clean Start means:

- no unnecessary musical lead-in before the intended word
- no buyer waiting for the message
- no music-first start by default
- front-end vocal-safe padding still exists
- fade-in handling still exists
- first word, breath, pickup, consonant, and vocal attack are protected

## Required padding

All LT-PIX-derived KUT candidates require padding before and after the vocal/content target to avoid abruptness.

This applies to mKs, sKs, and KKs when a clone-level KUT render is created.

## Front pad rule

Use scripted front-end-vocal-pad before the first intended vocal, word, phrase, or emotional target.

The front pad prevents chopped, mechanical, or abrupt starts.

Suggested starting values:

- mK: 80–150 ms
- sK: 150–300 ms
- KK: preserve section feel unless admin deliberately creates a shorter clone variant

The front pad may be silent, near-silent, room-tone-safe, or music-bed-safe depending on the source and product lane.

The front pad must not become a Musical INTRO unless deliberately reviewed and labeled as WITH_MUSIC_INTRO.

## Back pad rule

After the final meaningful word, consonant, breath, note decay, musical tail, or emotional resolution, preserve enough back padding to prevent abrupt cutoff.

Suggested starting values:

- mK: 300–600 ms
- sK: 500–900 ms
- KK: preserve musical or section resolution unless admin deliberately creates a shorter clone variant

## Protected ending rule

Protected endings are mandatory.

No KUT is approved if the final word, final consonant, breath, note decay, emotional tail, or musical resolution is clipped, rushed, or meaning-damaged.

## Twinkle / STI transition rule

Twinkle / STI is a signature transition component, not a peer KUT item.

After the content tail is protected, fade toward Twinkle / STI when the product lane requires it.

Current fade target: within about 1 second.

Evolution target: approximately 0.5 seconds when the VOC, decay, and emotional feel support it.

VOC controls the timing. The number is not absolute.

## VOC-based control

Fade and pad timing must be based on the vocal/audio condition, not a fixed number alone.

VOC checks:

- Does the first word breathe naturally?
- Is the first consonant preserved?
- Is the vocal attack intact?
- Is the vocal entry emotionally natural?
- Is the final word complete?
- Is the final consonant audible?
- Is the note decay protected?
- Is the emotional tail complete?
- Does the fade into Twinkle feel gentle rather than abrupt?

## Musical INTRO variant rule

Musical INTRO is not automatically bad.

A Musical INTRO becomes removable only when it is confirmed non-meaningful lead-in before the intended KUT word, phrase, vocal event, hook, or emotional target.

When the Musical INTRO adds emotional ramp, musical setup, drama, timing, or arrival, it may be preserved as a valid sibling clone variant.

## Variant outcomes

Each eligible mK and sK receives one START_VARIANT decision:

- CLEAN_START_ONLY
- WITH_MUSIC_INTRO_ONLY
- BOTH_VARIANTS_APPROVED
- NEEDS_ADMIN_REVIEW
- REJECT_START_DAMAGE

## Buyer-facing language

Default buyer option:

- Start clean

Optional buyer option only when reviewed:

- Start with a short musical lead-in

Do not show buyer technical language such as BLK1, contamination, trim boundary, or clone-level correction.

## Buyer checkbox rule

Do not show a buyer checkbox unless both variants actually exist, play correctly, preserve endings, and have passed admin/KKr review.

No fake buyer checkbox.

## Parent-source law

PIX, LT-PIX, KK, BLK, sub-BLK, and Song Section checkpoint remain source truth.

No parent source is destructively edited to fix a child KUT.

All intro removal, padding, fading, DP, tail repair, Twinkle/STI transition, and variant creation happen only on the derived KUT candidate clone/version, with provenance preserved.

## Final operational law

Default = no Musical INTRO.

Words first.

Music supports.

Clean starts must still breathe.

Protected endings must still resolve.

Twinkle / STI follows only after the vocal/content tail is safely preserved.

Offer Clean Start / With Music INTRO only when both reviewed clone variants exist.
