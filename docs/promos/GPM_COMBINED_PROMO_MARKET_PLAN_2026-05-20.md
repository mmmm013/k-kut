# GPM Combined Promo Market Plan — Viral + Mid-Range + Platform Path

## Status

Combined operating report for GPM / K-KUT / HUG promotional planning.

This report combines:

1. Viral promo model.
2. Mid-range promo model.
3. Platform/investor/grant implications.

## Executive Summary

GPM should run promos in two separate but connected lanes:

1. Viral promos create attention, sharing, and fast proof.
2. Mid-range promos create revenue, margin, and higher-trust fulfillment proof.

Together, they support the larger GPMx story: a music-services platform that turns real source music into governed private song moments, guided by occasion, feeling, and recipient context.

## The Operating Split

### Viral Layer

Purpose:

- draw visitors
- create attention
- make users hear audio quickly
- test emotional lanes
- generate shares and checkout clicks

Public standard:

> Hear it within 10 seconds.

Best offers:

- Birthday HUG
- I’m Sorry HUG
- Thank You HUG
- Encouragement HUG
- Thinking of You HUG
- Kids Birthday / KFS Singalong HUG

### Mid-Range Layer

Purpose:

- create real revenue
- support review and fulfillment
- increase average order value
- prove repeatable product packages
- support grant/investor credibility

Best offers:

- Wedding Track Pack
- Anniversary Pack
- Sympathy / Memorial / Celebration of Life Pack
- Business Client Appreciation HUG
- Family / Kids / KFS Pack
- Custom Curated HUG Bundle

### Platform / Long-Range Layer

Purpose:

- demonstrate GPMx as controlled infrastructure
- connect source music, KUTs, HUGs, MC-BOT, checkout, fulfillment, and delivery
- support patents, grants, partners, licensing, and future manufacturing/physical products

Do not lead public users with this layer. Use it for planning, investors, grants, and internal doctrine.

## MC-BOT Role

MC-BOT should be the chooser, not the explainer.

It should ask:

1. Who is this for?
2. What happened or what are you celebrating?
3. What feeling should they receive?
4. Soft, bright, grateful, romantic, apologetic, comforting, or strong?
5. Short HUG or fuller moment?

Then it should recommend one HUG.

## Key Risks

1. Too much internal language leaks onto public pages.
2. Checkout does not preserve selected KUT/source.
3. Viral pages lack visible audio.
4. Mid-range offers promise more customization than fulfillment can support.
5. Kids/KFS gets mixed into adult lanes without context.
6. Sympathy/grief language becomes too salesy.
7. Source-backed candidates are represented as fully governed KUTs before materialization.

## Doctrine

Customers may receive or purchase:

- one approved KUT
- one approved KUT-Combo
- one governed HUG/TUG delivery experience
- one reviewed mid-range package

Customers may not:

- rearrange song sections
- build custom edits
- stitch non-contiguous KUTs
- access PIX-PCK internals
- access CC backend records by default

## Final Recommendation

Keep the split:

- Viral gets people to hear and share.
- Mid-range gets people to buy and trust.
- Combined reporting proves the full market system.

Immediate operational focus:

1. Fix and verify checkout.
2. Make `/personal` promo-grid excellent.
3. Put Birthday, Apology, Wedding, and Anniversary in front of users.
4. Add KFS/Awesome Squad accordances without mixing child/family content into adult lanes.
5. Measure plays and checkout clicks.
6. Groom winners into mid-range packages.

Narrow rule:

> No public promo is real until a visitor can hear audio, understand the occasion, click one CTA, and reach the correct checkout path.
