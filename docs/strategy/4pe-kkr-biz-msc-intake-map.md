## 4PE Intake Procedure

4PE Intake is the first official control point.

Its job is to receive a possible asset, request, workflow, client idea, song use, HUG concept, or business opportunity and make it visible.

4PE Intake does not merely “collect” information.

It types, IDs, classifies, and initiates approval.

## Intake Responsibilities

4PE Intake should identify:

1. What came in.
2. Who or what it is for.
3. Whether it is business-facing, consumer-facing, internal, legal, music, retail, platform, support, fulfillment, or invention-related.
4. Whether it belongs to GPMx, K-KUT, GPEx, 4PE-BIZ, KKr, or another future subdivision.
5. Whether the asset is a song, track, excerpt, K-KUT candidate, HUG candidate, message, client request, delivery issue, support case, legal item, or operational process.
6. Whether it can move forward.
7. What approval gates apply.
8. What refinement loops must begin.

## ID Doctrine

Every meaningful unit should receive an ID.

The ID is not decoration.

The ID allows 4PE and KKr to track:

- origin
- owner
- business context
- music context
- approval state
- refinement state
- public-readiness state
- compliance state

Without IDs, the process becomes memory-dependent.

Memory-dependent business is risky.

## ACRONYM Syntax

The acronym syntax is part of the GPEx expansion strategy.

Initial syntax:

KKr-BIZ-MSC

Meaning:

- KKr = K-KUT refinement / routing / recognition connector
- BIZ = business context
- MSC = music-process context

This creates two initial subdivisions after KKr:

1. BIZ — the client/business classification
2. MSC — the music area classification

More subdivisions can be added as the system expands.

The syntax is intentionally expandable.

## Why BIZ Comes First

Inside each custom client or business process, KKr must first identify that the work is business-context work.

That is BIZ.

BIZ asks:

- Is this a client process?
- Is this a buyer path?
- Is this a business product?
- Is this a support or fulfillment case?
- Is this a licensing or rights concern?
- Is this an operational workflow?
- Is this part of a future scalable business line?

Once the business context is clear, the system can safely identify the more specific operating area.

## Why MSC Comes Next

MSC identifies the general area as music.

MSC asks:

- Is this connected to a GPMx song?
- Is this connected to an ASCAP registered track?
- Is this a K-KUT candidate?
- Is this a HUG candidate?
- Is this an approved song-section use?
- Is this a lyric, vocal, instrumental, chorus, verse, outro, or other music moment?
- Is this ready for public buyer exposure?
- Does it need refinement?
