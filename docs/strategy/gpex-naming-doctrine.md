# GPEx Naming Doctrine

## Locked Case Rules

These names must keep exact capitalization.

- GPEx
- GPMx
- K-KUT
- KKr
- 4PE
- 4PE-BIZ
- KKr-BIZ-MSC

Do not normalize these names into all caps unless Gregory explicitly changes the mark.

## Critical Rule

Always write:

GPEx

Never write:

GPEX

Always write:

GPMx

Never write:

GPMX

## Why This Matters

The capitalization is part of the identity system.

Mixed-case acronyms are intentional.

They support:

- trademark preparation
- naming discipline
- brand separation
- internal architecture
- public/private language control
- future viral expansion

## Typo Watch

Gregory may type quickly and may include accidental extra letters, especially random m/M or n/N characters.

Do not preserve accidental typo-noise in polished doctrine, public copy, legal copy, strategy maps, or repo documentation.

When meaning is clear, silently normalize to the locked name.
