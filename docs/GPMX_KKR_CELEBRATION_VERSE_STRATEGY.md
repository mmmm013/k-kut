# GPMx KKR Celebration Verse Strategy

## Purpose

This strategy controls how KKR finds K-KUT candidates for personal ceremony and celebration use.

For this strategy, KKR does not rely on official PIX song structure first.

Instead, KKR reviews:

- full verses
- verse subdivisions
- 2-line moments
- phrases
- statements
- idioms
- symbolic images
- final emotional lifts
- outro blessings
- exact CCs

The goal is to find feeling-bearing ceremonial units.

## Core Principle

KKR must cling to the feeling invoked by the language.

A candidate is valid only if it can help a sender say something meaningful to a recipient.

A K-KUT candidate is not merely an audio segment.

A K-KUT candidate is a sendable emotional ceremony unit.

## Silo Logic

Holiday-specific songs remain locked to their exact holiday.

Event-specific songs remain locked to their exact event unless explicitly approved.

Universal emotional PIX may be scored across multiple personal ceremony tags.

Separate love_family from love_romantic.

Do not allow romance-only candidates into family, public, honor, recovery, or sympathy ceremonies.

## Assessment Dimensions

Each verse, phrase, statement, idiom, expression, or CC candidate must be assessed for:

1. feeling clarity
2. positive meaning
3. recipient suitability
4. imagery
5. symbolism
6. microcosm value
7. ceremony fit
8. silo permission
9. audio viability
10. ending quality

## 10-Point Control Score

Score each candidate as follows:

- Feeling clarity: 0–2
- Positive / recipient-safe meaning: 0–2
- Imagery / symbolism / microcosm value: 0–2
- Ceremony fit: 0–2
- Audio cut viability / ending quality: 0–2

Total: 10

## Rating

- 9–10 = excellent
- 7–8 = strong
- 5–6 = possible
- 3–4 = triage
- 0–2 = reject

## Product Size Assignment

Product size is separate from occasion fit.

Use:

- Short KUT: exact CC, phrase, 2-line moment, short ceremonial statement
- HUG: full verse, chorus, bridge, compact section, or complete thought
- Big HUG: final chorus, outro, multi-part arc, or major emotional resolution

## Rejection / Triage Triggers

Reject or triage candidates with:

- weak ceremony value
- unclear feeling
- negative meaning
- dark or harsh imagery
- breakup context
- anger
- sarcasm
- romance-only mismatch
- too-specific story blocking broad use
- abrupt start
- abrupt end
- wrong silo
- no recipient-safe use

## Output Requirement

Every KKR verse / CC assessment should produce:

- PIX title / id
- source path
- verse or subdivision label
- candidate text or description
- start / end if known
- feeling tags
- celebration tags
- genre tag
- product size
- score out of 10
- rating
- risk tags
- review status
- review notes

## Control Statement

Do not approve based on filename alone.

Do not approve based on duration alone.

Do not approve based on one positive word alone.

Approve only when meaning, feeling, ceremony fit, silo permission, genre fit, and audio quality all support the candidate.
