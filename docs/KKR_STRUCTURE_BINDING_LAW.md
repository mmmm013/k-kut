# KKr Structure Binding Law

Status: LOCKED — OWNER AUTHORIZED  
Authority update: 2026-09-10

## Core law

KKr must prosecute every FullMix LT-PIX by the BLK strategy.

The binding order is immutable:

`FullMix SSOT → proven BLK map → exact KK/KOMBO CC → audio verification → heading → owner approval`

Structure is authority; the heading is post-capture metadata.

## Non-negotiables

- The KUT source inventory is 324 FullMix LT-PIX SSOTs.
- INSTRO-ONLY / INO-PIX inventory never feeds KUT production.
- One KK binds to exactly one proven BLK.
- One KK-KOMBO binds to two or more source-contiguous proven BLKs in original order.
- Noncontiguous and cross-source KOMBOs are forbidden.
- CC boundaries must be copied from locked BLK authority; CCs cannot create BLKs.
- Every capture comes directly from the original FullMix file.
- Every capture preserves source and rendered SHA256 lineage.
- No vocal may be cut mid-word, mid-phrase, or before its last vocal note.
- No capture may trespass into the next BLK.
- Duration never triggers or qualifies a KUT.
- Raw CCs remain internal and cannot be selected directly by customers.
- Traditional headings may be added only to verified KKs/KOMBOS.
- Unproven headings remain `BLK[n]`.
- KLEIGH uses `Refrain` only for a proven recurring chorus/title passage; every other section remains `BLK[n]`.

## Derivative order

KKs and KK-KOMBOs are established first. mKs are derived second. sKs are derived third.

mKs and sKs remain exact FullMix excerpts with BLK lineage and do not receive traditional song-structure headings.

## Stable identity

Internal keys and delivery identities remain stable after approval. Descriptive metadata may improve without silently changing source identity, BLK authority, CC boundaries, or audio hashes.

Existing Thank You internal identities remain:

`V1a`, `V1b`, `PreCh1`, `Ch1`, `V2a`, `V2b`, `Br`, `Ch2`, `Outro`.

Internal `Br` may display as `Bridge`; the internal identity remains unchanged.
