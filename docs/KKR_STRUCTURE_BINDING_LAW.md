# KKr Structure Binding Law

Status: LOCKED

## Core Law

KKr must consume, analyze, normalize, and organize every PIX by its own song structure.

Every customer-facing KK must bind to the owning PIX structure. Structure is not optional metadata.

## Non-Negotiables

- Each PIX may have its own structure.
- Each KK is its own structure-bound item.
- Structure labels must survive from lyric/SSOT layer into delivery manifests.
- Customer-facing HUG/K-KUT choices must be KKs only.
- CCs are internal only and can never be pulled or used directly by users.
- mKs, mKUTs, micros, generic audio scans, and arbitrary public/audio files are forbidden as customer HUG choices.
- Intro-only is forbidden.
- Intro inside a contiguous vocal KK-Kombo is allowed only when SSOT-contiguous.
- KK-Kombos must be contiguous.
