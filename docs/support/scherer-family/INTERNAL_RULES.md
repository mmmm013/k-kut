# Scherer Family Support Fund — Internal Rules

## Purpose

Direct voluntary support for Michael Scherer and family.

## Required Rule

No public language may promise:

- profit sharing
- repayment
- ownership
- royalty participation
- investment return
- sponsor return
- product delivery in exchange for support

## Approved Words

Use:

- support
- family support
- voluntary support
- help
- contribution
- care

Avoid unless legally reviewed:

- charity
- donation campaign
- investment
- profit share
- dividend
- return
- sponsor payout

## Separate Lanes

Keep separate:

1. K-KUT / HUG product purchases
2. G Putnam Music artist/catalog activity
3. Wounded & Willing mission support
4. Scherer Family Support Fund
5. Any future legally reviewed sponsor-sharing program

## User POV

A supporter must immediately understand:

- I am helping Michael Scherer and family.
- I am not buying a product.
- I am not investing.
- I am not owed anything.
- The money will be tracked and forwarded.

## GPEx Value Rule

Caring, Sharing, and Baring come first.

Profit comes second.
