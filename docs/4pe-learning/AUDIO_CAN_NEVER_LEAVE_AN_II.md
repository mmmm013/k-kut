# Audio Can Never Leave an II

## Locked Rule

AUDIO CAN NEVER LEAVE AN II.

Any public buyer-facing audio must be customer-ready II delivery audio.

That means:

front padding + approved audio + back padding + Twinkle / signature end sound.

## Public Buyer Audio Law

Buyer pages may not expose:

- raw KK audio
- source PIX audio
- guide audio as product audio
- INSTRO
- unfinished samples
- placeholder audio slots
- audio without padding
- audio without Twinkle

## Required Public Audio Path

Public buyer product audio should resolve through:

/ii-delivery/

and should carry a delivery marker such as:

bookend-twinkle

## Use-Case Rule

Hub BIC PASS is not enough.

Every public buyer use-case page with send/buy/checkout/order intent must have:

- customer-ready II audio
- working Stripe checkout
- no placeholder text
- no forbidden internal terms
- production audit PASS
