# KKr Intake / Ingestion / SLDS Doctrine — Current Prototype

## SLDS?

SLDS? = Sensible? Logical? Doable? Suitable?

Every KK, alt-KUT, art object, or feature candidate must pass SLDS? before buyer-facing use.

A single KK can serve multiple use lanes if the fit is right. A KK may be suitable for Father’s Day, Personal, Holiday, Gratitude, Legacy, Support, Repair, or other lanes at the same time. There is no one-use limit, but exposure balance, buyer clarity, approval status, and quality control still apply.

## Source Authority Rule

No outside files are allowed under any context.

All music, audio, lyric, melody, feeling, visual, and related ingestion must come from G Putnam Music-controlled sources or approved source-authority lanes.

For music-related KKr:
- Sources must connect to ASCAP-registered tracks.
- Sources must be published at least by G Putnam Music, LLC.
- If the required material is not available, the system must build it, replace it, or work around it reasonably.
- The two governing goals are Accuracy and Quality.

## Major Operation Results / IIs and CIs

### A. PIX / LT-PIX / IN-PIX / O-PIX

PIX is the music/melody/feel/auditory/emotion lane.

PIX digestion must capture:
- Music
- Melody
- Feel
- Auditory identity
- Emotional signal
- Energy level
- Positive, negative, and neutral signals
- Stress/calm indicators
- Lyric/text connection when applicable

LT-PIX and IN/O-PIX must inform each other, but they remain separate and unique lanes.

Rule:
- LT-PIX and IN/O-PIX are separate but equal for digestion.
- IN-PIXs can inform LT-PIX KKr digestion.
- IN/O-PIXs are not ignored, but they are not automatically public K-KUTs.
- For K-KUT buyer-facing use, LT-PIX-derived KKs and approved KK candidates remain the main route.

### B. Alt-KUTs / Small Units

KKr must also identify smaller or alternate KUT forms.

Current unit types:
- mK = mini-KUT
- LFL = LineFeel
- LPR = LinePair
- LTR = LineTrio
- TRM = single word / term
- XCLM = exclamation
- PHRZ = phrase
- Hook
- CTCH = catch-phrase

Definitions:
- LPR: two-line emotional or lyrical unit that can stand as a paired phrase.
- LTR: three-line emotional or lyrical unit that works as a triad.
- TRM: one word or term with emotional, thematic, or brand value.
- XCLM: short exclamation or vocal burst, such as “yeah,” “go,” “now,” or similar.
- PHRZ: phrase-level unit longer than a term but shorter than a full KK.
- Hook: repeated or highly memorable musical/lyrical anchor that defines recognition, emotional return, or singable identity.
- CTCH: idiom, colloquialism, metaphor, adornment, reinforcement, or memorable phrase with reuse value.

Alt-KUTs may become KPIs, metadata signals, routing aids, internal training objects, or future product candidates. They are not automatically public buyer-facing KKs.

## KK Review Method

KKr must run at least two passes.

### Pass 1 — LT-PIX / Per-Title Pass

Run the KKr pass against the LT-PIX or source title.

Purpose:
- Identify all KKs, BLKs, KK-Kombos, hooks, and relevant smaller units from that LT-PIX.
- Preserve provenance.
- Capture source structure.
- Verify audio boundaries.
- Identify title-linked thematic possibilities.

### Pass 2 — Title-Blind Theme Pass

Run a separate pass on pre-existing or newly created KKs using theme metadata rather than title.

Purpose:
- Identify emotional use cases that are not obvious from title.
- Detect Father’s Day, Mother’s Day, holiday, Personal, support, legacy, repair, gratitude, grief, celebration, and other use cases.
- Capture positive, negative, and neutral signals.
- Detect stress/calm levels.
- Detect gender-aimed pronoun issues for holidays.
- Route the KK into all suitable lanes.

## Dedup Rule

Do not duplicate an LT-PIX-derived KK as a separate solo KK.

If the same KK is found through both:
1. LT-PIX title/per-title search, and
2. title-blind KK-theme matching,

then it remains one KK with multiple metadata tags.

Correct handling:
- One KK record
- One audio object
- One source/provenance chain
- Many possible use lanes
- Many metadata tags
- No duplicate public listing unless intentionally packaged as a different buyer experience

## Holiday and Personal Population

After Father’s Day, KKr should populate:
1. Other holidays, each separately
2. Personal lanes
3. General HUG lanes
4. Specialty domains
5. Future package lanes

Each holiday needs its own SLDS? gate.

Gender-aimed holidays require pronoun/recipient detection:
- Father’s Day must avoid wrong-recipient feminine aim.
- Mother’s Day must avoid wrong-recipient masculine aim.
- Neutral holidays and Personal lanes can use broader recipient logic.

## TPR Requirement

More KKs require TPR when audio boundaries, source proof, or title-blind theme use are not yet reliable.

TPR must support:
- structure proof
- audio proof
- blank-slate listening
- KK boundary confirmation
- source-authority confirmation
- approval gate decisions

No KK should become buyer-facing solely because a title sounds right.
