# Theme Feed + MC-BOT System

## Locked Rule

All public buyer themes must be fed by governed KKr / II / MC-BOT metadata, not one-off page guesses.

## Why

PIX metadata helps, but it is partial.

KK metadata must carry most buyer-match weight.

MC-BOT must help buyers narrow choices without exposing raw inventory or internal labels.

## Core Flow

KKr labels and scores.

II registry prevents duplicates and tracks reusable delivery objects.

Theme feed decides where candidates can appear.

MC-BOT asks buyer-safe narrowing questions.

Public UI shows Featured first, then More, then More like this.

BIC production audit decides release.

## MC-BOT Role

MC-BOT is involved in every mature buyer theme.

MC-BOT should ask what feeling the buyer wants to send, who it is for, and whether the tone should be simple, deep, funny, tender, strong, romantic, or private.

MC-BOT routes to Featured, More, More like this, or one more question.

MC-BOT never exposes admin-only labels, forbidden inventory, or unreleased candidates.

## Theme Feed Layers

occasion, holiday, calendar season, relationship, POV, buyer identity, emotion level, phrase meaning, life stage, use case, promo suitability, exclusion law, weekly exposure.

## Revenue Doctrine

The site must be browseable and returnable.

Stable home and buyer hubs stay above rotating pages.

Seasonal and holiday pages rotate underneath.

Under-layers strengthen appeal without cluttering the top screen.

Example: Father’s Day → More → Cowboy / Western Dad.

## Release Doctrine

No public release until concrete KK / II candidate, delivery audio exists, padding + Twinkle together, approved Stripe path, route configured, and production BIC audit PASS.
