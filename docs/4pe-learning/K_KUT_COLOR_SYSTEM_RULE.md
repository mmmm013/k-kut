# K-KUT Color System Rule

## Locked Rule

Do not ban colors.

K-KUT base brand should be consistent:

- brown
- amber
- tan
- warm black
- gold

Decorative colors are allowed when they serve the moment:

- red for hearts, romance, warmth, urgency, or frames
- seasonal color accents
- celebration colors
- excitement colors
- image/artwork colors

## BIC Rule

BIC gates should ban unsafe content and unsafe audio behavior, not colors.

Continue banning:

- placeholder public copy
- missing audio states
- raw PIX public exposure
- raw KK public exposure
- mini-KUT / mK public buyer exposure
- runtime Twinkle assembly
- Supabase source audio on buyer pages

Do not ban colors globally.
