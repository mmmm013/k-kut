# 4PE-BIZ MSC / TT / BIZ Transfer Doctrine

## Locked Structure

4PE-BIZ-MSC is the active music-sector proving ground.

4PE-BIZ-TT is the Transfer Translator.

4PE-BIZ-BIZ is the generalized business operating system.

## Core Loop

MSC proves.
TT translates.
BIZ generalizes.
VOC aims.
DMAIC improves.
BIC gates.
PKK governs.

## MSC Role

MSC handles music-sector proof work, including PIX, KK, KKr, II, DP, HUG, K-KUT, Twinkle, bookends, audio law, and customer-ready audio delivery.

MSC is where pressure is highest and proof is strongest.

## TT Role

TT converts MSC-specific lessons into reusable business doctrine.

TT prevents MSC knowledge from staying trapped in music language.

TT also prevents BIZ from becoming vague consulting language without proof discipline.

## BIZ Role

BIZ receives translated lessons and turns them into generalized business standards, audits, training, client tools, and operating doctrine.

## Example Transfer

MSC rule:

AUDIO CAN NEVER LEAVE AN II.

TT translation:

A customer-facing deliverable cannot leave containment until it is finished, packaged, traceable, QA-approved, and delivery-safe.

BIZ rule:

No customer-facing output leaves the process until it passes BIC gate.

## Production Lesson From 2026-05-30

Internal review/proof materials must not be public.

Private staging must not be required by production deployment.

Finished delivery objects may be public only after BIC-level QA/QC.

This lesson was proven in K-KUT and must transfer into 4PE-BIZ-BIZ.
