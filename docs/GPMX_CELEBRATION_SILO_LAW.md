# GPMx Celebration Silo Law

## Core Rule

Holiday songs and event-specific songs are silo-locked.

They may not be used as general celebration candidates unless explicitly approved.

## Silo-Locked Examples

- Mother’s Day songs stay in Mother’s Day.
- Father’s Day songs stay in Father’s Day.
- Birthday songs stay in Birthday.
- Anniversary songs stay in Anniversary.
- Christmas songs stay in Christmas.
- Patriotic / national holiday songs stay in their specific national holiday.
- Recovery-specific songs stay in Recovery unless approved for broader encouragement.
- Memorial / remembrance songs stay in remembrance / sympathy / honor unless approved.

## General Celebration Scan

A general celebration scan may use only:

- universal emotional PIX
- non-holiday PIX
- non-event-locked PIX
- general love / gratitude / encouragement / honor / comfort / achievement material

## 4PE / KKR Application

4PE / KKR may still create K-KUTs from every section, chorus, final chorus, outro, and exact CC of every PIX.

But candidate use is controlled by silo fit.

A valid K-KUT candidate must pass both:

1. audio / section quality
2. occasion / silo permission

## Control Law

Do not use title words alone to approve a candidate.

Evaluate:

- PIX purpose
- lyrics / meaning
- section context
- emotional fit
- occasion specificity
- recipient suitability
- ending quality
- risk tags

## Product Rule

Short KUT / HUG / Big HUG are product sizes.

Holiday / celebration silo controls where those products may be offered.
