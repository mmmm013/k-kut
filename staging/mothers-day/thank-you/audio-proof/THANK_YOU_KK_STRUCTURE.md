# PKK-KK-EXPAND-002 — Thank You Controlled KK Structure

## VOC Lock

KKs are controlled song-section audio objects.

CCs are customer-facing Celebration Card meanings that may exist inside a KK.

No mKs.
No mini-KUTs.
No BB-BOT Story duplicate.
No public exposure until audio proof passes.

## Core Rule

KK = song-section audio object.

CC = emotional / customer-facing meaning supported by the KK.

One KK may contain multiple CC opportunities.

A chorus may contain multiple CCs because each chorus has sub-phrases.
