# PKK-004E — Twinkle Design Spec

## VOC Lock

Twinkle is a required audible HUG element.

Twinkle must be treated as important as KK and mK inside HUGs.

Twinkle is not copy-only. It is an audible emotional / sonic marker that supports the Celebration Card meaning.

## Current Direction

Primary exploration direction:
Option D — Natural Bird / Living Sound.

Twinkle is closing-only for now.

Twinkle may be used as:
- closing signature cue

Twinkle may NOT be used yet as:
- opening cue
- transition cue
- confirmation cue

## Preferred Source

Preferred source class:
Best candidate from PIX INO / IN-PIX audio when available.

Reason:
A Twinkle should feel owned, alive, and organically tied to the GPM / K-KUT sound world rather than generic stock audio.

## Current Proof Status

Twinkle model requirement: PROVEN
Dedicated Twinkle audio asset: NOT PROVEN
Option D direction: SELECTED FOR EXPLORATION
Closing-only rule: PROVEN IN SPEC
Public-ready Twinkle: NO

## Option D — Natural Bird / Living Sound

Use for:
- closing-only HUG signature
- Birthday HUG close
- Anniversary HUG close
- Mother’s Day / Thank You HUG close, if approved
- future sync candidates where organic sonic markers are needed

Feeling:
alive, real, organic, intimate, surprising, memorable

Risk:
may feel unrelated unless the selected sound is musically compatible, emotionally warm, and short enough to behave like a signature.

## Candidate Rules

Save candidates if they are:
- short or easily cut short
- emotionally warm
- distinctive
- not harsh
- not distracting
- compatible with KK audio endings
- usable as a closing signature
- potentially useful for sync

Reject candidates if they are:
- noisy beyond repair
- too long without a clean moment
- harsh / alarming
- too literal or comedic
- unrelated to the HUG feeling
- legally/source unclear

## Required Candidate Metadata

Each Twinkle candidate must track:
- source file
- candidate title
- why it may work
- known risks
- intended use
- proof status
- sync potential

## Proof Required Before Use

Before Twinkle can be marked PROVEN:
- source file exists
- candidate is staged
- candidate metadata exists
- listening approval completed
- closing-only placement rule approved
- HUG assembly rule identifies Twinkle as closing signature
- CC model updated from NOT_PROVEN to PROVEN or APPROVED

## Control Rule

No Birthday, Anniversary, or future HUG should be marked public-ready until Twinkle is selected and verified as audible as the closing signature.
